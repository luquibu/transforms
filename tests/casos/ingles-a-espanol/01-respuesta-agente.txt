Great question! I've gone through the codebase and here's what I found.

The sync job is actually failing because the `RefreshToken` worker runs in parallel for every account, which basically triggers Mercado Libre's rate limit. It might be worth considering running the refresh sequentially per instance, with a configurable delay between calls.

I also noticed that the retry policy treats 429 as terminal, which is probably not what we want.
